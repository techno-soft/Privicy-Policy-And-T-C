This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and what
you can do about it.

## The short version

- Your workouts, goals, measurements and history **stay on your device**. We do
  not upload them, and we cannot see them.
- We never sell your data, and we never use health data for advertising.
- Signing in tells us only who you are — an account identifier and an email
  address — not what you do in the app.
- You can delete your account, and everything with it, from inside the app.

## What we collect

### Account information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, the provider gives us:

- a unique account identifier,
- your email address (with Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide this, and
  we will receive a private relay address instead),
- your display name and profile picture, where the provider supplies them.

We never receive or store your Google or Apple password. Authentication is
handled by those providers and by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC).

### Health and fitness information

If — and only if — you grant permission, the app reads from Apple Health (iOS)
or Health Connect (Android):

- step count,
- active energy burned,
- body weight,
- height,
- distance,
- workout records.

With your permission the app also **writes** workouts you complete back to
Apple Health or Health Connect, so they appear alongside your other activity.

You can refuse this permission, or withdraw it later in your device settings,
and the app will continue to work. Health access is used solely to show your
activity and progress inside the app.

**We do not use health data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it,
and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.**

### Information you enter

Your profile, goals, workout plans, logged sets, body measurements and workout
history are created by you inside the app.

### Analytics

The app records anonymous usage events — which screens are opened, which
features are used — to understand what is working. Before any event is sent, it
passes through a filter that strips identifying and health-related fields,
including email addresses, names, weight, height, BMI, age, gender, heart rate,
steps, calories and body measurements. These fields cannot leave the app in an
analytics event.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, browsing history, or
advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising.

## Where your data is stored

**On your device.** Your profile, goals, workouts, history, progress and
measurements are stored in a database on your phone. They are not uploaded to
us or to anyone else.

**With our providers.** Your account identity is held by Firebase
Authentication (Google LLC), which may process it outside your country. Google
acts as our data processor under its own terms.

> **Note for a future release.** The app contains groundwork for optional
> cloud backup of workout data, which is **not active** — nothing is uploaded
> today. This policy will be updated, and you will be told, before any such
> feature is switched on.

## Why we are allowed to process it (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Where this legislation applies:

- **Performance of a contract** — account data, so the app can work at all.
- **Consent** — health data, which is "special category" data and is processed
  only on the explicit permission you give through your device. You can
  withdraw it at any time in your device settings.
- **Legitimate interests** — anonymous analytics, to improve the app.

## How long we keep it

Device data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
Account data is kept until you delete your account, after which it is removed
from our authentication provider.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Delete your account and all associated data** from inside the app —
  Profile → Delete Account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
- **Withdraw health permissions** at any time in iOS Settings → Health, or
  Android Health Connect.
- **Request access to, correction of, or export of** your personal data by
  contacting us.

Because your fitness data never leaves your device, uninstalling the app
removes it. Export it first if you want to keep it.

## Children

FitNexa is not intended for anyone under 13, and the app does not permit an age
below 13 to be entered. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you
believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store or Google
Play. **We never receive your card or payment details** — the stores handle
payment and tell us only whether a subscription is active.
